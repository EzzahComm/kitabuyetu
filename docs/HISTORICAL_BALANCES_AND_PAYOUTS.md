# Opening balances and member payouts in Kenya

## Historical balance validation

A member can have KES 12,000 in savings at cutover while the group's bank account holds KES 7,000 and the rest is lent to members. A bank statement can verify the KES 7,000. The signed savings register establishes each member's claim. Comparing the sum of savings directly with bank cash would give the wrong answer. Keep a historical claim, a confirmed cash asset and the amount currently available to withdraw as separate figures.

1. **Agree the closing date and scope.** Use the last completed accounting day in Africa/Nairobi. Agree which group, KES accounts and members are covered, including former members with balances. Record the last legacy entry and the first live entry. Identify transfers in transit, pending reversals and transactions already posted in Kitabu Yetu. Assign each transaction to one period.
2. **Obtain independent evidence.** Retain the signed closing trial balance, member schedules and a statement for each bank or mobile-money account covering the cutoff. Use loan schedules to distinguish principal outstanding from accrued interest and future interest. Use paid-up capital at the approved valuation for shares. Include only member-attributable welfare in a member schedule. Keep pooled welfare, other assets, liabilities and reserves on the group reconciliation.
3. **Reconcile both levels.** For each member and category, roll the previous verified close forward using supported movements and corrections. Compare the result with the signed member schedule. Sum the members in each category and match its control in the signed trial balance to the cent. Separately check total debits equal total credits and reconcile each cash control to its provider closing statement with a documented list of timing differences. Equal group totals can conceal money assigned to the wrong member.
4. **Resolve exceptions before acceptance.** Track each missing document, duplicate, out-of-period transaction, unexplained sign or variance with its source line, owner and resolution. An unresolved legacy identity stays in the exception register. Keep the batch on hold. Obtain a corrected source or documented reconciliation and rerun the whole batch. A balancing adjustment entered solely to obtain a match defeats the check.
5. **Approve one exact version.** For publication, require two distinct authorized group officials, such as the treasurer attesting the prepared batch and the chair independently approving it. Bind both attestations to the same group, closing date, source document hashes, exact CSV file hashes and validation result. Any change requires revalidation and fresh attestations. Use a one-time, group-scoped onboarding window and an atomic publication with a uniqueness constraint so concurrent requests and retries publish once. Keep evidence access restricted and record approvers, times, variances and member disputes in an append-only audit trail.
6. **Publish a read-only opening position.** Label it with its date and source on the statement. Exclude it from payout eligibility, lending limits, revenue, new contributions and receipts. If the group later transfers opening cash under platform control, recognize that cash once from an authenticated provider settlement, reconcile it to the identified opening asset and obtain approval for the member allocation. This transfer cannot create a second savings entitlement. Preserve the original close when a correction is approved and link the superseding version or adjustment to it.

### Using the balance checker

In **Data Import → Check historical balances**, a user with the financial import permission (`import.start`) and the historical import entitlement selects a closing date and uploads three independently prepared UTF-8 CSVs. The API entry point is `POST /api/v1/import/historical-balances/check`. Use the displayed group ID, the same ISO date and KES in every row. Use the platform membership number in both member files. Maintain a reviewed crosswalk from legacy register IDs when the formats differ.

| CSV field      | Contents                                                                                | Columns                                                            |
| -------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| `balances`     | Proposed opening position, one row per membership and category                          | `group_id,as_of,currency,membership_no,category,amount`            |
| `member_close` | Independent signed member schedule, prepared from the closing register                  | `group_id,as_of,currency,membership_no,category,amount,source_ref` |
| `controls`     | One total per category taken from the signed closing accounts, including explicit zeros | `group_id,as_of,currency,category,amount,source_ref`               |

Use a distinct document and line locator in each evidence row, such as `loan-register-p12-total-principal`. The categories are `savings`, `share_capital`, `loan_principal`, `loan_interest` and `member_welfare`. Use nonnegative outstanding amounts with up to 13 whole digits and two decimal places. Report debt in its own loan category. Negative or exceptional positions need an accountant's review. Each file supports up to 5,000 rows and 1 MiB. Larger groups need a whole-group review before acceptance, because checking unrelated slices could miss duplicates and omissions.

The checker verifies membership in the authenticated group, membership check digits, completeness in both directions, duplicate rows, source locators and exact amounts per member and category. A mismatch rejects the batch with its file and record when available. A match returns counts, category totals and SHA-256 hashes of the exact uploaded CSV bytes. Repeating a check has no financial effect.

**What the result establishes.** The files agree arithmetically and the memberships resolve. An official must still establish who issued each original, verify its authenticity, confirm the crosswalk and cutoff, reconcile the complete trial balance and provider statements, and resolve disputes. A copied schedule can match perfectly. The checker stores no files or approvals and supplies no import job or payout authority. The one-time publication and attestation workflow above is a proposed next stage.

## Members without personal phones

- Use the existing immutable group membership number for attribution. Keep contact details, a login credential and a payout destination separate. For a new member without a phone, the recommended enrollment path is officer-assisted identity and group-roster verification, with a second official reviewing the linkage. Issue the membership reference privately on paper or through a verified, consented channel. The current member creation and roster import still require a phone. Hold unmapped records pending assisted enrollment and never invent a number or reuse an officer's login or OTP.
- For a contribution paid from another person's phone, use the beneficiary's membership payment reference on PayBill. Keep the actual payer and the credited member distinct, retain the confirmed provider reference, protect payer details, and provide a receipt and dispute process. An invalid or ambiguous reference goes to the unallocated queue. A payer's contribution gives them no authority to receive the member's payout.
- For a payout, obtain the member's consent and verify ownership of an eligible bank account or mobile wallet using the provider's supported process. Offer an in-person verification and statement-collection path to a member who cannot use SMS. Contact changes and bank-detail changes need separate verification, an audit event and renewed payout approval. If the member has no verified destination, keep the entitlement and defer dispatch. A nominee or guardian arrangement would require its own mandate and policy.

## Automated B2C rails

| Rail                        | Intended use                                                                                                        | Kitabu Yetu status                                                                                                                                     |
| --------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Safaricom Daraja M-Pesa B2C | Pay an eligible verified M-Pesa wallet                                                                              | Existing member payout path, covered by local provider-mock tests                                                                                      |
| KCB Buni API                | Initiate a KES bank payout to the member's verified account through the group's contracted KCB disbursement product | Selected bank integration. Confirm the contracted transfer types, beneficiary verification, reference and final status semantics in the KCB sandbox    |
| Equity Jenga API            | Initiate a KES bank payout to the member's verified account through the group's contracted Equity transfer product  | Selected bank integration. Confirm the contracted transfer types, beneficiary verification, reference and final status semantics in the Equity sandbox |

For each bank, establish the supported bank destinations, required customer identifiers, authentication, request signing, callback verification, provider status inquiry, reversals, fees, cutoffs and statement access from its issued contract and sandbox documentation. A receiving bank account may avoid a personal wallet requirement. Any phone requirement for the particular transfer product still applies. [KCB Buni developer portal](https://buni.kcbgroup.com/) · [Equity Jenga developer portal](https://developer.jengaapi.io/)

Use one payout ID across rails, bind the approved amount, currency, verified beneficiary, funding account and chosen rail to it, and reserve available funds once. Persist the attempt and its unique reference before dispatch. Treat an API acknowledgement as pending. Confirm final success through an authenticated provider callback or status inquiry that matches the expected instruction and beneficiary, amount, currency and provider transaction ID. Settle the reservation and post once in the same database transaction. Deduplicate provider transactions within their documented reference namespace. Reconcile gross, net, fees and reversals to bank statements daily.

If an acknowledgement or callback is lost, keep the reservation and query the original provider using the original reference. A timeout cannot authorize a second rail. After definitive non-payment, reconcile and close that attempt before an officer authorizes a new attempt. Record a later reversal separately with a link to the original settlement. For both banks, exercise success, rejection, timeout followed by late success, repeated callbacks, mismatched beneficiary or amount, fees and reversal in the sandbox before enabling dispatch.
