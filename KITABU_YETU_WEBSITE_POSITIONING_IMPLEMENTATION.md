# KITABU YETU WEBSITE POSITIONING UPGRADE

Implement the following changes to the existing Kitabu Yetu website.

## CORPORATE POSITIONING

Position Kitabu Yetu as:

THE DIGITAL OPERATING SYSTEM FOR COMMUNITY ORGANISATIONS.

Kitabu Yetu is an impact-driven technology company building digital infrastructure that helps community organisations, NGOs, cooperatives, churches, associations, CBOs and development programmes manage operations, strengthen accountability, improve communication and connect to opportunities for sustainable growth.

Technology is the foundation. The development ecosystem is the complementary growth layer.

Do not position Kitabu Yetu as a charity, NGO, bookkeeping-only application or financial institution.

## HOMEPAGE

Replace narrow bookkeeping-focused messaging with:

Headline:
The Digital Operating System for Community Organisations.

Description:
From community groups and cooperatives to NGOs and development programmes, Kitabu Yetu provides the digital tools to manage people, finances, communication and programmes—strengthening accountability and creating opportunities for sustainable growth.

Primary CTA: Explore Our Platform
Secondary CTA: Talk to Our Team

Introduce two strategic dimensions:

1. Technology Platform
2. Development Ecosystem

## ABOUT US

Use the following content direction:

Kitabu Yetu is an impact-driven technology company building digital infrastructure for community organisations and the institutions that support them.

We believe that strong communities are built on well-managed organisations. Yet many community groups, cooperatives, associations and development initiatives continue to rely on fragmented systems, manual records and disconnected communication processes.

Kitabu Yetu is addressing this gap through practical, accessible and integrated technology that helps organisations manage their people, finances, activities and programmes with greater efficiency, transparency and accountability.

Our platform supports organisations at different stages of growth—from small community groups seeking reliable records to NGOs and development programmes managing portfolios of multiple organisations.

Beyond technology, we are building a connected ecosystem that helps organisations discover opportunities, strengthen capacity, access relevant services and build meaningful partnerships.

Our goal is to make digital transformation practical and accessible to the organisations that drive community development.

## VISION

A future where every community organisation is digitally empowered, financially accountable and connected to opportunities for sustainable growth.

## MISSION

To equip community organisations and the institutions that support them with accessible digital technology that strengthens organisational management, financial accountability, communication and programme impact.

## CORE VALUES

1. Accountability – Transparency, responsible management and trust.
2. Inclusion – Accessible digital tools for organisations of every size.
3. Innovation – Technology that responds to real organisational needs.
4. Integrity – Responsible data management and ethical partnerships.
5. Collaboration – Cooperation between organisations and institutions.
6. Impact – Technology that creates measurable organisational value.
